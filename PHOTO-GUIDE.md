## Tips

- **JPEGs** are best for photos (smaller file size). Use `.jpg` extension.
- **PNG** for the 1% for the Planet logo (transparent/logo format).
- If your photo filenames already match, you can rename them in Finder: right-click → Rename.
- The website will show the images automatically once the files are in `assets/img/`.
