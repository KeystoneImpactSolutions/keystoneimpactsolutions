What does "grant ready" actually mean?

Grant readiness is your organisation's capacity to receive, manage, report on and sustain funding once it lands. The Grant Readiness Self-Assessment checks it across five areas, each with the question funders are really asking:

  • Financial: can you produce reliable financial information on demand?
  • Operational: do you have the people to deliver what you're promising?
  • Compliance: can you meet the conditions attached to the money, as well as the criteria to win it?
  • Documentation: could you show how every dollar was spent, tonight, without digging?
  • Leadership: does your board treat financial stewardship as part of the mission?

Plus ten yes-or-not-yet questions to answer with your leadership team, real requirements from international funds and Australian foundations, and a checklist for deciding whether to apply at all.
