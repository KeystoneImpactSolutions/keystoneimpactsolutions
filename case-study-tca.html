From Reporting Overwhelm to Operational Clarity for Tenkile Conservation Alliance

Sector Conservation & Biodiversity
Engagement Ongoing operational support and impact strategy
Services Fundraising operations, impact reporting, automation, communications support
Location Australia & Papua New Guinea
Published 26 March 2025
The Starting Point

Critical Conservation Work. Buried in Operations.

Tenkile Conservation Alliance is a conservation organisation working to protect critically endangered tree kangaroos and support community-led conservation initiatives in Papua New Guinea.

When the organisation's leadership came to Keystone Impact Solutions, they were under intense operational pressure. Essential annual reports were overdue, manual tasks consumed valuable time, and scattered systems made it difficult to focus on strategic conservation work.

The challenge wasn't commitment or expertise — it was capacity.

The Core Problem

The COO didn't start a conservation organisation to spend her days buried in paperwork. But the operational burden was beginning to overshadow the mission. That's exactly the gap Keystone Impact Solutions is built to fill.

The Challenge

Capacity, Not Commitment

  • Overdue annual reports and compliance documentation
  • Time-consuming manual administrative tasks
  • Limited bandwidth for strategic conservation leadership
  • Fragmented systems draining focus and energy
The Role

Embedded Operational & Impact Support

Keystone Impact Solutions stepped in to provide ongoing operational and impact support, working closely with the team to stabilise systems, complete critical reporting, and streamline day-to-day operations.

The focus was not just on catching up, but on creating sustainable systems that would reduce pressure long-term and free leadership to focus on conservation outcomes.

Operational & Reporting Support

  • Completion of three outstanding annual reports
  • Development of a formal booklet for the Papua New Guinea Government
  • Ongoing support for compliance and reporting requirements

Systems & Automation

  • Automation of repetitive administrative tasks
  • Streamlining internal workflows using AI and digital tools
  • Reducing duplication and manual effort across operations

Communications & Fundraising Support

  • Support for online shop content and communications
  • Improved social media and supporter engagement
  • Strengthening internal clarity around priorities and workflows
The Outcome

Focus Restored. Mission Reclaimed.

Operational Outcomes

  • Annual reports completed and compliance restored
  • Time-consuming tasks automated and streamlined
  • Clearer systems supporting day-to-day operations

Organisational & Personal Outcomes

  • Significant reduction in operational overwhelm
  • Greater clarity on priorities and responsibilities
  • Leadership bandwidth reclaimed for mission-critical conservation work
  • Renewed energy and focus for the work that matters most
"Jess is multi-talented, using AI and tech tools to make work streamlined and efficient. We've seen a real boost in our shop sales and socials, as well as completing three outstanding annual reports and a booklet for the PNG Government."
Jean Thomas — COO, Tenkile Conservation Alliance. "Reliable, knowledgeable, outstanding."
The Bottom Line

Systems Stabilised. Mission Centred.

Before working with Keystone Impact Solutions, Tenkile Conservation Alliance was weighed down by overdue reporting, manual tasks, and operational complexity — leaving little space for strategic conservation leadership.

Today, the systems behind the organisation are stronger, clearer, and more manageable. The operational burden has eased. Time has been reclaimed. And the focus is back where it belongs: protecting biodiversity and supporting conservation on the ground.
